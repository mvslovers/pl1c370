# Tier 17 rules, measured against PL/I (F) V5.5

Measured 2026-10-12/13 on MVSTK5-BLD. Probes (Q1PIC, generated by `genpic.py`, JOB00288; Q2PCTX, JOB00290). The test
programs `tests/pli/t87pica` to `t92pic3` print what PL/I (F) prints;
with Tiers 0 to 16: 94 programs identical (JOB00300 and JOB00301; t7div
again in JOB00303, see the end).

## Editing

Ninety pictures and values (Q1PIC: the Language Reference's figures in
section D and more), each assigned from FIXED DECIMAL(15,5) and written
with `PUT EDIT ... (A)`, give the rules `rt/pl1pic.c` follows; the host
test `tests/host/t_pic.c` checks all ninety:

- The value is converted to FIXED DECIMAL with the picture's digits and
  its scale (digit positions after the V); high-order digits are lost
  (`99999` from 1234567 is `34567`, `9V9` from 12345.67 is `56`), the
  fraction is truncated.
- 9 Y T I R always print (Y a zero as blank; T I R overpunch: `999I`
  1021 is `102A`, `99T9` -1021 is `10K1`). Z and * suppress leading zeros
  of the integer part; the fraction is shown unless the whole value is
  zero, and when every digit position is Z, * or drifting and the value
  is zero, the whole field is blank (or asterisks), a static `$` with it
  (`$ZZZV.ZZ` 0 is seven blanks, `**/**/**` 0 eight asterisks).
- `, . /` print after a significant digit; otherwise as the digit
  position before them (`*` after an asterisk, else a blank), and with
  no digit position before them at all, as themselves (`V.999` 0.5 is
  `.500`). A point right after the V prints when the fraction does
  (`ZZV.ZZ` 0.03 is `  .03`), one right before it only after a
  significant integer digit (`ZZ.VZZ` 0.03 is `   03`). B is a blank.
- `$ S + -` once are static, more than once a drifting string: its first
  character is no digit position, the symbol goes right before the
  first significant digit of the string, or at its end when those
  digits are zero -- an insertion character after the last drifting
  character belongs to the string (`$$$,999` 12 is `   $012`,
  `$$,$$9V.99` 0.07 is `    $0.07`, `--,--9` -1234 is `-1,234`).
- S gives `+` or `-`, `+` gives `+` or a blank, `-` a blank or `-`; CR
  and DB print when the value is negative.

## Two values

A numeric picture has its characters and its arithmetic value (Q2PCTX,
t91pctx):

- PUT LIST writes the characters, PUT DATA without quotes (`P= $12.50;`),
  as does an A format; F, E and P formats take the value. A character
  picture is a string: PUT DATA quotes it (`CP='AB$C12';`).
- `||`, LENGTH (7 for `$$$9V.99`), SUBSTR and assignment to a string take
  the characters.
- Arithmetic and comparison take the value, also against a string, which
  becomes FIXED DECIMAL(15,0) as in Tier 5: `P = '12.5'` is false for
  12.50, `P = 12.5` true. Two pictures compare by value.
- A string assigned to a picture is converted at the picture's scale
  (`'7.25'` to `$$$9V.99` is `  $7.25`).

PL1C/370 decides the view in `lower.c` (pic_expr): the characters where a
string is wanted, B_PICVAL (de-editing, `pl1_pic_value`) elsewhere. An
assignment edits the value as FIXED DECIMAL(15,q) (`pl1_pic_edit`).

## Character pictures

X, A and 9 positions. A source that does not fit raises CONVERSION,
ONCODE 618, with the whole source as ONSOURCE (`|ABCDE1      |` for a
CHAR(12) source); a normal return without a change is IHE799I. `$` is
valid in an A position. PL/I (F) refuses such a constant at compile time
(IEM1814I).

## The P format

`P'picture'` edits a FIXED or FLOAT value as an assignment to such a
picture would (3.75 with `P'**9V.999'` is `**3.750`; PL1C/370 truncates
a FLOAT to the picture's scale, as an assignment does -- not measured
with a value that needs it).

## PL/I (F) at compile time

A constant assigned to a picture is converted by the compiler, and one
that loses high-order digits is a severe error (IEM1806I, RC 12; the
first form of Q1PIC, JOB00287) -- the probe assigns through a variable.

## Not done

Floating-point pictures (E, K), the scaling factor F, sterling pictures
(out of scope), a drifting string past the V, Z and * in one picture,
GET into a picture or with a P format, a picture as an argument by
reference, STATIC INITIAL of a numeric picture.

16 with a listing mvsMF could not read; t7div ran identical alone in
JOB00303.
